The LF Edge project Akraino is preparing for its upcoming Release 9 , which will mark a major step toward enabling Physical AI , the convergence of artificial intelligence, edge computing, and real-world interaction. What Is Physical AI? Physical AI represents a new phase in intelligent systems, combining recognition, prediction, and action in the physical world. Unlike purely digital AI systems, Physical AI connects sensing and decision-making with tangible movements and real-time feedback...
